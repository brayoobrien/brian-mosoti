SOP — Weekly Lead Research
Step 1

Open the Lead Tracker sheet and filter by 'Pending' status.

Step 2

For each entry, verify the company URL and capture the decision-maker's name.

Step 3

Add verified contact details into the assigned columns. Leave blanks where unsure.

Step 4

Mark the row as 'Ready for QA' and notify the reviewer in Slack.

Step 5

Log completion time and any blockers in the daily standup note.

Training Note — Image Annotation Basics

What you're doing: drawing a tight box around every car in the image so the model learns what "car" looks like.

Rules: the box must touch the edges of the car — no extra space, no missing wheels. If two cars overlap, label them separately.

When unsure: skip the image and flag it in the QA channel. It's better to skip than to guess.

Goal: 95%+ accuracy. Speed comes after accuracy — never before it.
